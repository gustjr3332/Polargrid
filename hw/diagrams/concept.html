PolaGrid control module concept (v0.2)

Left: front view of one control module from the room side. Right: section through the cell (thickness exaggerated, not to scale).

Light pathL1 (fixed) then L3 (rotating) set the transmitted light by cos squared of the angle. L2 is crossed with L1 so the four corners stay dark at every angle.
DriveThe 2:1 gear turns the disc half the servo angle, so a 180 deg servo covers 0 to 90 deg. No hub or gear touches the window. L2 sits 0.3 mm in front of the L3 disc.
ProductOne central control module (ESP32, desk sensor, Wi-Fi) plus control modules sold one cell at a time. The user sticks as many modules on the window as needed. The demo is one central module and one control module.
Not measured yetGear backlash, light lost in the 2 mm acrylic carrier, real transmission versus angle. All go into the 10-point test.
