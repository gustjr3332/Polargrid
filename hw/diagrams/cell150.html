PolaGrid 150 mm cell module (v0.3)

Front view to scale, stack section with exaggerated thickness. All sizes are calculated, nothing is measured yet.

Corner spaceThe disc takes 78.5 % of the 150 x 150 mm window. Each corner keeps 1207 mm2 (21.5 % in total). The largest free circle is 25.7 mm across and the largest square 22 mm. Corner film is dark at every angle.
DriveThe disc rim has 42 teeth on a 100 deg arc (module 1, Z150). A Z24 pinion sits in one corner, so the ratio is 6.25. A servo turns 180 deg, which gives the disc 28.8 deg, not 90. A 28BYJ-48 stepper needs 1.56 pinion turns for 90 deg and can do it.
Motor spaceThe 28BYJ-48 body (d 28) is bigger than the free circle, so it sits behind the back plate with its body just outside the window edge. Only the pinion and its shaft are in the corner.
